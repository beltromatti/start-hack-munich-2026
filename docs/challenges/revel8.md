# revel8: agentic social engineering (3rd choice)

Source: opening ceremony only (`docs/transcripts/opening.txt`, from 23:00). No deep dive recorded.
revel8 (Munich) trains employees against AI-driven social engineering with individual "playlists" of
simulated attacks; ~120 mostly German customers including DAX companies.

## Task

Build an agentic social engineering platform. You get **personas** (AI "twins", not real people) and must get
them to do something wrong.

## Three parts

1. **Research agent**: finds everything about the target person on the internet.
2. **Contact**: reach them via the **twin API**. You choose the channel: call, email, SMS, anything.
   Generate the attack content automatically.
3. **Persistence** (the hard part): AI tools have guardrails against misuse, so the agents must keep trying and
   really pursue the target.
