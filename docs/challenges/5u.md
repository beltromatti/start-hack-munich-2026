Source: 5U AI deep dive, 21 Nov 2026 (`docs/transcripts/5u-deep-dive.txt`). A challenge document follows.
5U AI (Munich) builds "AI workers" for freight forwarders.

## Setting

You are a freight forwarder run entirely by AI agents.

## The manual process today

1. A customer emails: e.g. "2 boxes, 500 kg, from China to Munich, what does it cost?" (an RFQ, request for quotation).
2. The forwarder asks carriers (airlines and others) for rates.
3. It picks the best rate, adds its margin, sends the customer a quote.
4. If the customer accepts, the forwarder books with the carrier using the **rate ID**.
5. Later the customer asks: "Where is my shipment?"

## Where rates come from

- An API.
- A browser portal that protects itself against scraping.
- Email to the carrier; sometimes a PDF rate sheet from which you work out the price yourself.

## What to build

- A **router** that sorts incoming mail into three kinds.
- **RFQ agent**: reads the request, gets rates, prepares and sends the quote.
- **Tracking agent**: books after acceptance, answers status questions.
- **Rate ingestion agent**: rate sheets arriving by mail go into your pricing module.
- Carrier agents to contact carriers.
- A **CRM** that logs quotes, margins and gives insight.
- A view where a human sees what the agents are doing and can step in.

## Setup

- Your own mailbox via their **mailbox API** (Swagger docs). No Gmail, no SMTP.
- From about **15:00**, a new request **every 6 minutes**, non-stop through the night.
  The reply must go out within those 6 minutes.
- No test emails beforehand. Leaderboard data available as JSON.
- All LLM calls go through **their LLM gateway** (details announced).

## Twists

- The system changes without announcement (extensions of the current setup).
- Tricky emails; emails with missing information where you must ask the customer.
- They said other teams attacking your mailbox is "a possibility".
- No GDPR requirement.

## Scoring

- Leaderboard: weighted mix of **accuracy**, **price closeness to the best price**, **speed** and **LLM cost**.
  Shows how many of your replies were accepted.
- **Top 5** on the leaderboard go to the jury: code base, UI/UX, pitch.

## Prizes

1. Each team member flies a plane. 2. Concert tickets. 3. Dinner with the founders.
