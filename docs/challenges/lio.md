# Lio: procurement and supply chain (1st choice)

Source: Lio deep dive, 21 Nov 2026 (`docs/transcripts/lio-deep-dive.txt`). A handout follows on Discord.

## Setting

- You are a startup. You just signed your first customer, **SUNY**, which works with the game studio
  **Popstar Studios**.
- Their game **Grand Vice 6** goes on sale **Sunday 20:00**. Your job: make sure everything runs cleanly until then.
- Lio employees role-play the SUNY team.

## Products

- Standard edition and collector's edition (with extras such as a leaflet and a statue).
- Parts come from different suppliers and go through different packing lines.
- Many units are already assembled; some parts still have to be delivered.

## Process

1. A buyer orders in SAP (purchase order).
2. The supplier checks inventory, maybe sources from its own supplier, confirms it can ship, ships.
3. Some deliveries are tracked.
4. Goods are received at the **warehouse in Hamburg**, packed and distributed for sale.

## Today

- Only purchase orders are in SAP. Everything else: spreadsheets, emails, phone calls.
- Suppliers are worldwide; mails in e.g. Korean, Chinese, Polish.
- Some shipments are not in the master data, only in a loose spreadsheet.

## Suppliers

- **6 suppliers**, shipping by sea and air to Hamburg.
- Each has its **own mailbox and replies** to what you send.
- Treat them as real business partners. Spamming them annoys them.
- To email them, register your sending address in the portal.

## What can go wrong

- Late deliveries, stuck in a port, unclear status.
- Wrong parts or wrong versions delivered.
- External events: geopolitics, earthquakes.
- Examples named: a supplier changes its bank account; someone gets hacked.
- Data changes throughout the weekend; events are announced on the portal dashboard.

## Portal

- Register a team (name + password), add team members.
- Dashboard with events and announcements, a data page, an API (key in settings).
- **Office hours** with SUNY personas, booked in the portal: IT, senior buyers, head of procurement.
  They know things that are not in the data. Prepare questions. Slot times may change.

## Deliverables

- Prototype (deployed is nice, not required), pitch deck, demo video, code.
- Must show agentic behaviour. Business model is up to you.
