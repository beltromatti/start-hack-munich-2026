What Zauber does

Zauber builds purpose-built AI agents for sea and air freight forwarders. The agents work inside the operator's inbox and connect to CargoWise One and other core systems, automating rate requests, bookings, milestone and data updates, and document handling while keeping operators in control. Zauber Technology GmbH is based in Berlin, Germany and was founded in 2024 by Erik Muttersbach, co-founder and former CTO of Forto. The product is live with leading global freight forwarders across more than 40 country branches.

Agents for the shipment lifecycle

Pre-configured agents cover quotation, booking, milestone tracking, document extraction, exception handling, and SOP monitoring. Agents are configured in plain language and take effect instantly, with session monitoring and adoption tracking by branch and user.

Integrations

Deep integrations ship out of the box: CargoWise One and other transport management systems, rate management systems, Microsoft Outlook and 365, SharePoint and OneDrive, Teams, and Slack. Human oversight and full auditability are built in, so forwarders automate what they trust and review the rest.

Security and compliance

Zauber is GDPR-compliant, built and hosted on European infrastructure with data residency in the EU. The architecture is ISO 27001 aligned with certification in progress, multi-tenant, encrypted, access-controlled, and independently security tested.

Measured impact

Forwarders report up to 88 percent time saved per task, from five to eight minutes down to under one minute, around 90 percent low-touch bookings at top-20 forwarders, and full automation of milestone creation at the scale of tens of thousands of milestones per month.
