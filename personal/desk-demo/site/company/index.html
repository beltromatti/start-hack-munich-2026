What it does with one request

Most of a forwarder's day is mail: a request comes in, the airlines are asked, one price goes back. The desk does that typing. On the right is an example request, from the first mail to the booking.

  1. Request came in14 cartons to Dubai, with a packing list as PDF06:23:10
  2. Read the mail and the packing listIt also reads a scan, a spreadsheet or a voice message06:23:14
  3. Compared 3 offersEvery total is added up by code, never by the model06:23:41
  4. Quote sentOne option, one total, in the customer's language06:23:57
  5. Booked after the customer said yesWith the airline. If the price moved, it tells the customer06:44:02

When it was unsure, it says so

It does not wait for a person. If something does not add up, it decides, tells the customer and leaves a note on the desk.

Control

What it cannot get wrong

The agent judges a request the way a person would. Four things are not left to its judgement. They are built into the system under it.

In the desk you add people and set their rights. Switches for what the agent may do on its own are there as a draft.

  • Every price and date a customer reads is computed by code.
  • A mail goes only to people in that thread and to carriers on our list.
  • A customer's thread sees only that customer's shipments.
  • After a restart nothing is lost and nothing is answered twice.

Plug in

Start with your rate sheets

It reads rates the way your airlines send them: PDF, Excel, Word, plain text or a photo of a printout. Mail runs through the challenge's mailbox today. Outlook and Gmail are not connected yet.

It prices the next weight break too

299 kg can cost more than 300 kg. It checks the next break and takes the cheaper total.

An API for your own tools

The desk you see reads the same endpoints you would.

# every shipment, newest first
GET  /api/deals
# one shipment with its mails and every step
GET  /api/deals/{id}
# a question in plain words
POST /api/ask

A price lookup for the assistant you already use

The rates are reachable as an MCP server. Add the address below to your assistant and ask it for a route. It runs on the demo rate sheets.

# add as a remote MCP server
https://milan-desk.samsonntag11.workers.dev/mcp

# tools
price_route(destination, origin?)
routes_with_one_airline(origin?)

Two tools, read only. No customer data behind it.
